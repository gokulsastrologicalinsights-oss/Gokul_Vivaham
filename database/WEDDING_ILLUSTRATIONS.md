# Wedding illustration assets

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the homepage example cards.
These represent invented couples, not customer endorsements or verified marriages.

Prompt set: three landscape, waist-up portraits of fictional adult South Indian couples in Tamil wedding attire; maroon, green, and blue bridal sarees respectively, cream/gold groom outfits, soft wedding backgrounds, natural expressions, no text or logos.

Public files: public/couple-illustrations/couple-1.png through couple-3.png.
